# Seller guide

## Create your shop

1. Open `/seller/signup`, add your contact details, Ghana WhatsApp number, shop location, and an established social or website link for verification.
2. Choose your Mobile Money network and enter the number and account name if you accept Mobile Money. Select cash on delivery if you offer it. You can change these later.
3. Read the seller agreement and prohibited-items list, accept the agreement, and create your account. Check your email if Supabase asks you to confirm it.
4. Sign in at `/seller/login`. Your shop starts as pending while the marketplace team checks the profile. You can prepare drafts while it is pending.

## Set up the public shop

Open **Shop profile** in the seller studio. Add your logo, banner, bio, location, WhatsApp number, social link, and payment options. Save the profile. Your shop page and products appear to customers after the shop is approved and active.

The profile page shows your public shop link and QR code. Copy the link into your social bios or save the QR code to print. Customers can browse without creating an account. Their order requests open a WhatsApp conversation with your shop; payment is made directly to you.

## Add and manage products

1. Choose **Add products** and select one or more photos of the same item. The photos are grouped into one product gallery. You can also take a photo with your phone camera.
2. Optionally request AI suggestions. Check the title, description, colour, and moderation pre-check yourself. The AI does not set your price or stock and does not approve the listing.
3. Choose a category, enter a GHS price, and add every size and its available stock. Save the draft for review.
4. Open **Products** to add or remove photos, update the name, description, price, and variant stock; hide or show a product; duplicate it as a new draft; mark all variants sold out; or delete it. Changing photos, name, or description sends the listing back to review. Price and stock updates keep its current approval.
5. Read any reviewer note on the product card. Fix the listing and submit a corrected version if it was rejected.

New drafts are hidden from customers until approval. Do not list counterfeit goods as genuine, weapons, illegal drugs, stolen goods, explicit adult content, hazardous items, or anything illegal in Ghana.

## Orders and renewals

Open **Orders** to review order items and customer details. Set each status to New, Confirmed, Paid, Delivered, or Cancelled, and add a private seller note for your team. Confirm delivery and payment with the customer in WhatsApp.

The dashboard and profile page show trial or subscription status. Contact the marketplace team to record a renewal. An expired shop may be hidden after the configured grace period.

## Install and share from Android

Open Charwin Stores in Chrome on your HTTPS domain and choose **Install app**. After installation, share product photos from another Android app to Charwin Stores. Shared images open as a draft for the signed-in seller. If the share option is unavailable, use **Add products**. iPhone users can upload photos from the Quick Upload screen.

Only the offline page and static app resources are cached. Product updates, order status, and uploads need an internet connection.
